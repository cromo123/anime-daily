Frequently Asked Questions

When does the daily challenge change?

Today follows your browser’s local calendar date. A new day is playable once its approved challenge is available. If a day has not been published, the site shows an unavailable message rather than substituting another date. Reload after midnight if an older page remains open.

How many questions are there?

Four categories with five chained comparisons each: 20 questions and a maximum score of 20. Each correct choice earns one point; wrong choices do not stop the chain.

Can I replay?

Yes. After completing a day, use the replay option. Your first official completed result stays fixed. Practice does not improve that stored score.

How does the archive work?

The game’s Archive tab lists stored, published days. You can play a day you missed or open a completed day’s result and replay it. Missing historical days are not generated just because you click them. Public recaps contain spoilers and become available after a day has ended worldwide.

What happens if I close the browser?

Official answers are saved on the server, and the anonymous browser cookie lets the site restore progress. Blocking or deleting the cookie, using private browsing, or changing browsers can make that progress and history unavailable. There is no login or cross-device account sync.

Why are series and seasons treated differently?

More Episodes compares a connected mainline series. The other rounds compare the specific MAL entry shown, including its own score, members, or release date.

Why does a number differ from MAL or what I remember?

The catalog is imported data, not a live MAL lookup. Ratings and membership change, episode totals depend on stored series relations, and a sequel may have very different values from the first season. Our methodology explains these limitations.

What does “Top X%” mean?

It uses completed official results for that same challenge. Your rank is one plus the number of players with a strictly higher score; the displayed top percentage is your rank divided by completed players, rounded, with a minimum of 1%. Tied scores share the same standing. A single completion can display Top 100%.

Where can I report incorrect data or a bug?

Use Feedback. Include the challenge date, category, entry title or MAL link, and what you expected. Contact email is optional. Reports are private and are stored for review; submitting one does not send an email automatically.
