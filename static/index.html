An anime game worth thinking twice about

How well do you know your anime?

AniMoredle is a daily “more or less” game: choose which of two anime has the higher value, then see the factual result. Familiar favorites meet unexpected challengers across four rounds and 20 comparisons.

Four ways to compare

  • Higher Score — a specific entry’s MAL user score.
  • More Popular — its member/list-user count, with rank revealed too.
  • More Episodes — the full connected mainline series total.
  • More Recent — its complete start/release date.

One point, then a new challenger

Each correct answer earns one point, up to 20. The right-hand anime carries into the next matchup with its revealed value visible. Your first official completion is saved; later replays do not replace it.

Today follows your browser’s local date, with a new challenge when that day is published. The playable Archive holds available earlier days.

A sequel can have a different score or audience from the original. A familiar franchise can have a surprisingly recent entry. More Episodes deliberately asks a different question: how long is the connected series? These distinctions make the comparisons more than a test of title recognition.
