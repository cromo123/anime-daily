AniMoredle

About

AniMoredle is a daily anime “more or less” game. Each challenge asks you to compare anime across four categories:

  • Higher Score
  • More Popular
  • More Episodes
  • More Recent

Complete 20 comparisons, see how you scored, and return for a new daily challenge.

Familiar anime, surprising comparisons

A well-known title does not always have the highest score, and a recent sequel can be newer than you expect. Each round asks you to look at anime from a different angle, then reveals the stored facts behind your choice.

AniMoredle uses MyAnimeList-derived data and deterministic comparison rules. It is an independent game, not an official MyAnimeList product. Data and Methodology explains how the values and series totals work.
