Plain-English policy

Privacy Policy

AniMoredle uses an anonymous browser cookie to recognize a player. This supports saved challenge progress and results, including official attempt behavior. The identifier is not intended to contain your name or contact information.

Challenge activity

Official challenge answers and results are stored so AniMoredle can restore progress, save scores, show archive results, and calculate anonymous community statistics. AniMoredle does not currently offer user accounts and does not collect account passwords or payment information.

Technical information

Infrastructure and hosting providers may process normal technical request information, such as IP addresses and server logs, for security, reliability, and operation. AniMoredle does not collect precise location information.

Cookie choices

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Doing so may cause saved challenge progress, official results, and challenge history to stop working or become unavailable in that browser.

Advertising

AniMoredle may use third-party advertising services in the future. If advertising services such as Google AdSense are enabled, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those services are activated. Future advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies, but those advertising services are not active today.

Questions

For privacy questions, email contact@animoredle.com.
