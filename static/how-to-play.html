Your daily anime challenge

How to Play

AniMoredle asks a simple question: which of these two anime has more? A daily challenge contains four rounds, with five comparisons per round. You can score one point per correct answer, for a total of 20.

Choose, reveal, and carry forward

Select either anime card with a click or tap. Keyboard players can Tab to a card and press Enter or Space. The server checks your choice, then both values are revealed. Green identifies the correct anime; red marks an incorrect selection. The label “Your choice” shows what you selected.

The game advances along a fixed chain: anime 1 versus 2, then 2 versus 3, through 5 versus 6. The previous right-hand anime carries forward with its revealed value still visible, whether you were right or wrong. The new challenger’s value stays hidden until you answer.

After rounds one, two, and three, a checkpoint shows your round score. Press Continue when you are ready for the next category. After round four, your final result appears.

The four rounds

  • Higher Score: the displayed entry’s user score.
  • More Popular: the displayed entry’s member count.
  • More Episodes: the connected mainline series total.
  • More Recent: the displayed entry’s full start/release date.

Your official attempt

Official answers are saved as you play. Your first completed result for a day is your official score; a replay cannot improve or replace it. An anonymous browser cookie lets the site restore progress and recognize that result. Clearing it or switching browsers can make that history unavailable.

Results include your score and, when available, your standing among official completions. Breakdown lets you revisit all 20 comparisons, your choices, and available anonymous community accuracy percentages.

Today and earlier days

Today follows your browser’s local calendar date. A day is playable when its challenge has been published. Use the game’s Archive tab to play available days or revisit a saved result and choose a practice replay. The public historical archive also provides readable recaps, clearly marked with spoilers.
