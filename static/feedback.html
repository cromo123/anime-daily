Feedback

Found a bug, a confusing comparison, or something you would like to see? Send a private report. You do not need an account or an email address.

For incorrect anime data, include the title or MAL ID, category, and challenge date. A specific example helps distinguish a season’s value from a connected series total. Please avoid passwords, account details, or other sensitive information.
